================================================================================
WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES
================================================================================

INPUT:
  - Assessment results (student answers)
  - Question bank (question metadata)

PROCESS:
  1. Calculate statistics (correct rate, discrimination)
  2. Detect weak items using rules
  3. Generate explanations
  4. Categorize by risk level
  5. Present to admin

OUTPUT:
  - Flagged items with explanations
  - Risk levels (RED/YELLOW/GREEN)
  - Actionable recommendations
  - Quality metrics

EXAMPLE OUTPUT:

  Q47: TOO EASY (RED)
  • 98% of students answered correctly
  • Cannot differentiate abilities
  • Recommendation: REPLACE

  Q25: TOO DIFFICULT (RED)
  • Only 5% answered correctly
  • Possibly flawed or too advanced
  • Recommendation: REVIEW/SIMPLIFY

  Q256: GOOD (GREEN)
  • 48% answered correctly (optimal)
  • Good discrimination
  • Recommendation: KEEP
